# Street Hustle — Content Rating Notes

Use these notes when completing the official Google Play / IARC content-rating questionnaire. Do not select a rating manually; answer the questionnaire accurately and let the rating authority calculate it.

## Current content

- Fictional third-person life-simulation game.
- Non-graphic vehicle collisions can reduce a health stat.
- No blood, gore, dismemberment or realistic injury imagery is currently implemented.
- Fictional police pursuit, wanted levels, arrest and a simplified prison/release phase are present.
- Fictional risky-money choices are present, but the game does not provide real-world instructions for committing crimes.
- No gambling or real-money wagering is implemented.
- No alcohol, tobacco or drug-use gameplay is currently implemented.
- No sexual content or nudity is currently implemented.
- No strong/profane language is intentionally included in the current dialogue.
- No user-generated content, public chat or player-to-player messaging.
- No real-money purchases in the current build.
- No advertising in the current build.
- No real-world location collection.

## Intended audience positioning

The game is not designed specifically for young children because its life story includes financial pressure, police consequences, arrest and prison themes. Final audience/age settings must match the official rating returned by Play Console.

## Re-check before release

If future versions add stronger violence, weapons, substance use, stronger language, romance/sexual content, gambling-like mechanics, multiplayer chat or user-generated content, repeat/update the official content-rating questionnaire before publishing the update.
