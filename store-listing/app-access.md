# Street Hustle — App Access / Reviewer Instructions

The current build does **not** require an account, password, subscription, location or external hardware.

## Reviewer access

1. Launch Street Hustle.
2. The game opens directly into the playable world.
3. On touch devices use the on-screen directional pad, RUN, PHONE and E buttons.
4. The starting objective is to speak to Ma at home.
5. Progress is stored locally on the device.

No demo credentials are required.

## Important controls

- E / on-screen E: interact.
- P / PHONE: open in-game phone.
- J: missions/tasks.
- L: life progression.
- N: fictional local feed.
- T: taxi when at the taxi rank.
- Esc: pause / exit vehicle.

## Network

The Android release pipeline packages the engine and current character assets into the app. Review should not require a player account or Street Hustle backend server.
