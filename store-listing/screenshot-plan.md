# Street Hustle — Google Play Screenshot Plan

Use **real screenshots from the Android build**. Do not use concept art as if it were gameplay.

Recommended landscape screenshots, captured without browser chrome:

1. **Starting neighbourhood / main road**
   - Sbu visible in third person.
   - Houses, road, pedestrians and traffic visible.
   - Objective HUD visible.
   - Caption idea: `START FROM R0. BUILD YOUR LIFE.`

2. **Living neighbourhood**
   - Multiple walking humanoid pedestrians, taxi rank and shop area.
   - Caption: `A LIVING FICTIONAL SOUTH AFRICAN CITY.`

3. **Jobs and progression**
   - Tasks/Missions panel over actual gameplay.
   - Caption: `WORK. LEARN. BUILD SKILLS.`

4. **Driving**
   - Player driving the owned hatchback/taxi traffic visible.
   - Caption: `WALK, DRIVE AND MOVE AROUND THE CITY.`

5. **Business / money**
   - In-game phone Business or Money screen.
   - Caption: `SAVE MONEY AND BUILD BUSINESSES.`

6. **Weather / day-night**
   - Rain or evening lighting with streetlights.
   - Caption: `A WORLD THAT CHANGES WITH TIME.`

7. **Consequences**
   - Wanted stars/police chase, without graphic violence.
   - Caption: `CHOICES HAVE CONSEQUENCES.`

8. **ZERO → HERO loop**
   - Home/property upgrade or Life progression screen.
   - Caption: `RISE. FALL. REBUILD.`

## Capture rules

- Capture from a real Android APK/AAB build where possible.
- Hide any browser address bar or desktop taskbar.
- Do not show debug consoles, error banners, GitHub UI or development tabs.
- Avoid screenshots with visual bugs, overlapping NPCs or stretched vehicle parts.
- Keep marketing text accurate to features present in the uploaded build.
- Keep the privacy/LEGAL button acceptable if visible, but do not make it the focus.
- Use screenshots from at least one ordinary mid-range Android device before Production.
