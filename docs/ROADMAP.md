# Street Hustle Roadmap

## v0.1 — Day One

Goal: prove the 3D game is fun before expanding the world.

- Build one small 3D neighbourhood
- Add third-person player movement
- Start with R0
- Add player home
- Add one street
- Add basic houses and shops
- Add 3 NPCs
- Add 3 small earning opportunities
- Add one risky fictional opportunity
- Add money and reputation consequences
- Add save/load

## v0.2 — Daily Life

- In-game phone
- More jobs
- More NPCs
- Friendship/trust
- Clothing
- Basic inventory
- Day/night cycle

## v0.3 — Vehicles and Consequences

- Vehicles
- Driving
- Police/heat system
- Arrest consequences
- Housing progression
- Relationships

## v0.4 — Business

- Start small businesses
- Employees
- Expenses and income
- Business reputation
- Business failure/bankruptcy

## v0.5 — Zero → Hero → Zero

- Major life events
- Asset loss
- Prison/release states
- Long-term consequences
- Rebuilding after failure
- First complete rise/fall/rebuild arc

## Long-term

- Multiple male and female protagonists
- Shared city where character stories cross
- Football, music, technology, work, business and crime routes
- Character aging
- Family and children
- Larger city districts
- Multiple endings based on the whole life, not only money
